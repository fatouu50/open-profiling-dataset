## Investigation

**Detection delay factors** — **established**

The delay is not explained by linkage blindness: the offence series were linked to one another well before they were linked to a person, and the multiple press epithets record the successive stages of that linkage. The binding constraint was that no method existed to convert retained biological evidence into an identification when the offender was not himself in a criminal DNA database.

> This case, Ridgway and Bundy give the dataset three distinct delay mechanisms: absent technology here, institutional neglect and forensic limits in Ridgway, jurisdictional fragmentation in Bundy. They should not be collapsed into a single 'linkage blindness' explanation.

**Forensic methods used** — **established**

Investigative genetic genealogy (IGG), followed by direct DNA comparison for confirmation.

Sources: [8] [7]

> IMPORTANT DISTINCTION. IGG is contested on privacy and governance grounds, not on grounds of reliability. Erlich et al. (2018) showed that long-range familial search could identify a majority of individuals of European descent in the United States from third-cousin matches, and Guerrini et al. That is an ethical and regulatory objection. It is NOT the objection that applies to bite-mark comparison or polygraphy, which is that they do not work.
